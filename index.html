Speedy Assistant

A personal productivity assistant.

What this app does

Speedy is a private, single-user assistant that helps its owner stay organised. With the owner's explicit authorisation it can send email on their behalf, read their inbox for summaries, manage calendar events, and read or update specific spreadsheets and files the owner points it at.

Who uses it

This is a personal, non-commercial tool used only by its owner. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users.
