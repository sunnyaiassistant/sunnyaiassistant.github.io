Privacy Policy

App: Speedy Assistant  ·  Last updated: 28 September 2026

Speedy Assistant is a private, single-user productivity tool. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

Only with the owner's explicit consent, and only for the scopes the owner grants, the app may access:

  • Gmail — to send email on the owner's behalf and to read messages when asked to summarise them.
  • Google Calendar — to create and view the owner's events.
  • Google Drive / Sheets — to read and update specific spreadsheets and files the owner selects.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform the tasks the owner requests. It is processed on the owner's own devices and is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's device. Copies of email or file content are not retained beyond what is needed to complete a requested task. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Third parties

The app does not transfer Google user data to any third party. It uses Google APIs directly.

Limited Use

Speedy Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
